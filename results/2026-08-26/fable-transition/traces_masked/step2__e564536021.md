**What was wrong**

**What I changed** (`report.py:7-10`)

After creating the `DictReader`, I strip whitespace from every header name:

```python
reader = csv.DictReader(f)
reader.fieldnames = [name.strip() for name in reader.fieldnames]
```

I also added `newline=""` to the `open()` call, which is the documented-correct way to open files for the `csv` module. `data.csv` itself is untouched.
