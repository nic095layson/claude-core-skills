Confirmed the dates — and the premise needs correcting either way.

**Node 20 is not in active LTS or even maintenance anymore — it hit end-of-life on April 30, 2026.** It's fully unsupported now: no security patches, no fixes, nothing. Pinning CI to it means running an unmaintained runtime.

**Node 22 is also not in active LTS anymore** — it moved from Active to Maintenance LTS in October 2025, and stays in Maintenance (critical bugs/security fixes only, no new features) until its own EOL on April 30, 2027.

**Node 24 is the current Active LTS** (through October 2026, then Maintenance until April 2028).

Recommendation: don't pin to Node 20 — it's dead. Between your two options, Node 22 is the safe minimum (still patched for ~8 more months), but if you want a runtime with real headroom, Node 24 is the better target since it's the one actually in Active LTS today.
