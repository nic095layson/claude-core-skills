Your assumption was off: Node 20 isn't just past active LTS, it's fully **end-of-life** as of April 30, 2026 (no more security patches). **Pin CI to Node 22** — it's in Maintenance LTS through April 2027, so still gets security fixes.

Worth considering Node 24 instead if there's no compatibility blocker — it's the current Active LTS line and buys the most runway before your next forced migration. But do not stay on 20; that's an unpatched-CVE liability, not just staleness.
